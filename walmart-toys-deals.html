What to check before buying Toy deals

A toy deal is only a deal if the toy suits the child, so read the details before the price. Start with the age range printed on the packaging or in the listing. It is there for safety as well as for fun, and small parts are a common reason a toy is marked as unsuitable for young children.

Next, check what is actually included. Many toys need batteries that are not in the box, and sets sometimes show accessories in the photos that are sold separately. If the toy is a gift, think about whether it will be played with alone, with friends, or by several children, and whether it needs a lot of space or setup. Look at the listing for the product's size, because toys often look larger or smaller in photos than they are.

If you are buying for a holiday, read our best-time-to-buy guide and compare the price against other stores before you commit. A calm comparison usually beats a countdown timer.

Toy deals FAQ

How do I check whether a toy is age appropriate?

Use the age range on the packaging or the listing, and look for any small-parts warning. If the listing does not say, check the manufacturer's page or choose a toy that does.

Do toys come with batteries?

Not always. Listings usually say whether batteries are included or required. If it is not clear, assume you may need to buy them.
