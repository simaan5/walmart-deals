  1. Make the list now. Impulse is the house edge. Write down the 3–5 things you actually need, with the price each sells for today — that number is how you’ll know a real deal in the noise.
  2. Price-anchor this week. Check your items’ current prices so November’s “70% OFF” banners can’t fool you about what changed.

Honest FAQ

Are Black Friday discounts actually the year’s best? Sometimes. TVs and big electronics: usually yes. Appliances, tools and home: often matched at other sales (see our timing guide). Toys frequently drop again in mid-December. The trap isn’t buying on Black Friday — it’s buying something you didn’t price-check.

Do we mark up urgency? No. If we can’t verify a claim, we don’t make it — no fake countdowns, no “only 3 left” theater. Prices are stamped when checked and can change; that’s the only urgency that’s real.
