Walmart, Amazon, Target and Best Buy timing, with sources, and Walmart deals we have price-checked.

Retailer dates last checked against each retailer’s own announcement: Nov 2, 2026.

In short: Black Friday is Friday, Nov 27, 2026 (the day after Thanksgiving, Nov 26) and Cyber Monday is Nov 30. Walmart has announced all three of its events: Early Black Friday Nov 12-15, Black Friday Nov 23-29 and Cyber Monday Nov 30-Dec 2. Best Buy and Target have announced their early sales. Amazon has confirmed the two dates but not its sale window, so its dates are announced dates TBA.

Honest FAQ

Do I need Walmart+? No. Early Black Friday, the Black Friday event, and Cyber Monday are open to everyone. Walmart+ adds two things Walmart has spelled out: early access to hot deal drops at 12:01 a.m. ET on Nov 12 and Nov 23, and select online-only deals held for members for the first 24 hours, while supplies last. Whatever is left opens to all customers the next day (Nov 13 and Nov 24).

Are Black Friday discounts actually the year’s best? Sometimes. TVs and big electronics: usually yes. Appliances, tools and home: often matched at other sales (see our timing guide). Toys frequently drop again in mid-December. The trap isn’t buying on Black Friday — it’s buying something you didn’t price-check.

Do we mark up urgency? No. If we can’t verify a claim, we don’t make it — no fake countdowns, no “only 3 left” theater. Prices are stamped when checked and can change; that’s the only urgency that’s real.
